The U.S.–Iran fight over the Strait of Hormuz reaches a sixth day as Iran strikes Gulf bases and Trump vows to make America “the guardian of the Hormuz Strait”

+

The exchange previewed this morning has escalated into a sixth consecutive day. U.S. Central Command said it struck Iranian command centers, air-defense and missile sites near the strait early Thursday “to further degrade Iran’s ability to threaten innocent mariners,” and again disabled an oil tanker in the passageway. Tech · Semiconductors

A Shanghai startup unveils an HBM-free 14nm AI chip, claiming higher memory bandwidth than Nvidia’s H200 while sidestepping U.S. export curbs

+

Chinese startup Dongfang Suanxin (founded 2024, based in Shanghai’s Zhangjiang hi-tech zone) exited stealth and launched its flagship DF1000 processor at an event on Monday, July 13, ahead of the World Artificial Intelligence Conference in Shanghai. The chip is built on a mature 14nm process, yet the company claims 520 teraflops of BF16 performance and 6.4 TB/s of memory bandwidth — roughly a third higher than Nvidia’s H200 — achieved without high-bandwidth memory (HBM) or advanced nodes, via a “software-defined computing” framework plus 3D-stacked near-memory computing. Dongfang Suanxin closed a Series A+ in late April at a roughly 12.3 billion yuan (~$1.7 billion) post-money valuation, backed by the National AI Industry Investment Fund and the venture arms of Xiaomi and JD.com, and is led by Tsinghua semiconductor veteran Wei Shaojun. One caveat worth keeping front of mind: every performance figure here is vendor-stated, not independently benchmarked.

Space · Astronomy

Hubble reveals four white dwarfs hiding in plain sight within 65 light-years, concealed by brighter red-dwarf companions

+

A team from the University of Warwick and the University of Colorado Boulder (LASP) directly detected four white dwarfs in nearby binary systems for the first time, all within 65 light-years — one of them the ninth-closest white dwarf to the Sun. The systems (G 203-47, GJ 207.1, LHS 1817 and Wolf 1130) had each appeared to be single stars because a larger, brighter red dwarf outshone the compact stellar remnant. Using Hubble’s ultraviolet spectrograph plus custom calibration to separate the white-dwarf UV signal from red-dwarf flaring, the team confirmed all four; one system, G 203-47 at just 25 light-years, took 27 years after its initial radial-velocity wobble to pin down.

The work was published and circulated July 13–14, 2026. White dwarfs so close to Earth are prized natural laboratories — for studying stellar aging and for calibrating the cooling-age clocks astronomers use to date stellar populations across the galaxy.

Science · Zoology

Researchers formally describe a new monkey with orange lips and a froglike roar from the DR Congo — only the fifth new African monkey in 75 years

+

Scientists confirmed a new colobus monkey, Colobus congoensis (known locally as “likweli”), in the region of the Democratic Republic of Congo’s Lomami National Park, in findings published July 15, 2026. The roughly 15-pound (7 kg) monkey has conspicuous orange lips, slate-grey cheekbones framing black-rimmed eyes, silky black fur, and distinctive roaring and snorting vocalizations. The trail began with a 2008 expedition photo of an unidentified monkey, a second sighting in 2018, and then a dedicated search to describe it.

It is described as only the fifth new African monkey species named in the past 75 years. Given its restricted range, small population, and pressure from hunting and habitat loss, the authors recommend an IUCN Red List status of Endangered.
